ITDR is an emerging category focused specifically on detecting and responding to identity-based attacks — credential theft, privilege escalation, anomalous authentication.

## Key points

- Traditional security monitoring often focuses on network and endpoint signals, leaving a gap around purely identity-based attack indicators like impossible travel or unusual permission changes.
- ITDR tools correlate identity provider logs, authentication patterns, and privilege changes to detect compromise that wouldn't trigger traditional security alerts.
- This category has grown directly in response to attackers increasingly targeting identity infrastructure itself, rather than trying to bypass endpoint or network defenses.

## The takeaway

ITDR reflects a broader industry recognition that identity has become the primary attack surface — and needs detection capability built specifically for it.
