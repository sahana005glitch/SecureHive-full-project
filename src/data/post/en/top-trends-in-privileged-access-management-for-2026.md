PAM has moved well beyond simple password vaulting toward a broader identity security discipline covering human, machine, and now AI identities.

## Key points

- Zero standing privilege — removing default access entirely in favor of just-in-time grants — has moved from an advanced practice to an expected baseline.
- Unified platforms consolidating PAM, endpoint privilege management, and identity governance are replacing point-solution sprawl in many organizations.
- AI agent governance is emerging as a new PAM frontier, as autonomous agents increasingly hold their own credentials and take independent actions.

## The takeaway

PAM's evolution reflects a broader shift: identity, not the network perimeter, is now the primary security boundary organizations are defending.
