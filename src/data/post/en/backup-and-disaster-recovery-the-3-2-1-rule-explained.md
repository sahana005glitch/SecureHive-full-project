The 3-2-1 backup rule — three copies of data, on two different media types, with one copy offsite — remains a foundational, low-tech defense against ransomware and hardware failure alike.

## Key points

- Ransomware increasingly targets backup systems specifically, which is why an offline or immutable backup copy that can't be encrypted alongside production data is critical.
- Regularly testing backup restoration (not just confirming backups complete) catches failures before they matter during an actual incident.
- Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point Objective (RPO) should be defined and tested per system, since not every system needs the same recovery speed.

## The takeaway

A backup strategy is only as good as its last successful test restoration — untested backups are a hope, not a plan.
