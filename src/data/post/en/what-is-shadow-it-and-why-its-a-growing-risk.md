Shadow IT refers to software and services employees adopt without formal IT approval — often well-intentioned, but invisible to the organization's security controls.

## Key points

- Cloud-based SaaS tools have made shadow IT dramatically easier to adopt, since employees can sign up with just a work email and a credit card.
- Data uploaded to unsanctioned tools falls outside the organization's data loss prevention and backup policies entirely.
- A reasonable shadow IT response includes visibility tools (CASB) combined with a fast, low-friction approval process for legitimate new tool requests.

## The takeaway

Shadow IT usually isn't malicious — it's employees solving a real problem faster than official channels allow, which means the fix involves faster official channels, not just enforcement.
