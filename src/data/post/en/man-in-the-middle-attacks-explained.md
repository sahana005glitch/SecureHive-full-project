A man-in-the-middle attack positions the attacker between two communicating parties, allowing them to eavesdrop or alter the communication without either party noticing.

## Key points

- Public Wi-Fi networks are a common setting for MITM attacks, since attackers can position themselves on the same network as the target.
- TLS/HTTPS is designed specifically to prevent MITM attacks by cryptographically verifying the identity of the server being connected to.
- Certificate pinning adds an extra layer of protection for mobile apps by rejecting connections even to fraudulently-issued certificates.

## The takeaway

The widespread adoption of HTTPS has made classic MITM attacks harder, but they remain a real risk on unsecured networks and misconfigured systems.
