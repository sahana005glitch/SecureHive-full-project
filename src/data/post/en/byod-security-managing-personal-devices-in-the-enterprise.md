Bring-your-own-device policies offer convenience and cost savings, but blur the line between personal and corporate security responsibility.

## Key points

- Mobile Device Management (MDM) solutions let organizations enforce security policies (encryption, screen lock, remote wipe) on personal devices accessing corporate data.
- Containerization approaches separate corporate apps and data into an isolated profile, limiting what corporate policy can see or control on the personal side of the device.
- A clear BYOD policy addressing what happens to corporate data when an employee leaves — including on their personal device — closes a common gap.

## The takeaway

BYOD security is as much a policy and expectation-setting exercise as it is a technical one.
