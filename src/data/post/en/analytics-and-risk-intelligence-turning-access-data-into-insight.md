Most organizations generate enormous amounts of access and authentication data, but few extract meaningful risk insight from it without dedicated analytics capability.

## Key points

- Risk scoring models that combine access level, unusual behavior, and asset sensitivity help security teams prioritize which anomalies actually deserve investigation.
- Visualizing access patterns across an organization can reveal unexpected relationships — like an account with access spanning departments it has no business reason to touch.
- Predictive risk analytics increasingly aim to flag risky access patterns before they're exploited, not just detect exploitation after the fact.

## The takeaway

Raw access data is only valuable once it's turned into prioritized, actionable insight — which is the specific gap risk intelligence tools aim to fill.
