Insider threats come from people who already have legitimate access — employees, contractors, or partners — making them harder to detect with traditional perimeter-focused tools.

## Key points

- Insider threats split into malicious (deliberate harm) and negligent (accidental exposure) categories, requiring different detection approaches.
- User and Entity Behavior Analytics (UEBA) tools flag unusual access patterns, like a user suddenly downloading far more data than their role typically requires.
- Offboarding processes that fail to revoke access promptly are one of the most common — and preventable — insider threat vectors.

## The takeaway

Reducing insider risk isn't about distrust of employees; it's about limiting the blast radius of any single compromised or careless account.
