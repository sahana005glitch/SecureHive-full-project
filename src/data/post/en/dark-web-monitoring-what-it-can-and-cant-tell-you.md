Dark web monitoring services scan underground forums and marketplaces for mentions of an organization's data, credentials, or brand — providing early warning of compromise.

## Key points

- Finding employee credentials for sale on the dark web often indicates a breach at a third-party service, not necessarily a direct compromise of the organization itself.
- Monitoring coverage varies significantly between providers, since dark web forums require ongoing infiltration and access that isn't uniformly available.
- Dark web alerts are most valuable when paired with a clear action plan — an alert without a rapid credential rotation response provides limited protective value.

## The takeaway

Dark web monitoring provides early warning, not prevention — its value depends entirely on how quickly the organization acts on what it finds.
