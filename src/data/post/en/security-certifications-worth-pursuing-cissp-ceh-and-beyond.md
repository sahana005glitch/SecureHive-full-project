Security certifications vary widely in focus, difficulty, and industry recognition — choosing the right one depends heavily on career direction.

## Key points

- CISSP is broad and management-oriented, often required for senior security roles, while requiring several years of documented experience to obtain.
- CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker) and OSCP focus more narrowly on offensive security skills, with OSCP generally regarded as more hands-on and rigorous.
- Cloud-specific certifications (AWS, Azure, GCP security specializations) have grown in relevance as more infrastructure moves to cloud platforms.

## The takeaway

The right certification depends on where you're headed — management track, technical specialist, or cloud-focused — more than which one is broadly 'best'.
