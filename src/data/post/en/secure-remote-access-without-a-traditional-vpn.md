Zero Trust Network Access has emerged as a VPN alternative that grants access to specific applications rather than broad network-level access.

## Key points

- Unlike a VPN, which typically grants access to an entire network segment, ZTNA grants access only to the specific application a user is authorized for.
- ZTNA solutions typically evaluate device posture and user identity continuously, not just at initial connection, aligning with broader zero trust principles.
- Because ZTNA doesn't expose the internal network directly, it significantly reduces the lateral movement risk that comes with traditional VPN access.

## The takeaway

ZTNA addresses a structural weakness in traditional VPNs: broad network access granted after a single authentication event, regardless of ongoing risk signals.
