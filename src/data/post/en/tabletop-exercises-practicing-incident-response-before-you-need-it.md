A tabletop exercise walks a team through a simulated security incident scenario verbally, testing decision-making and communication without any actual systems being affected.

## Key points

- Effective tabletop exercises include realistic scenario injects that force participants to make decisions with incomplete information, mirroring real incidents.
- Including non-technical stakeholders (legal, PR, executive leadership) in tabletop exercises tests the full incident response chain, not just the technical response.
- Documenting gaps identified during the exercise — and actually following up on them — is what separates a valuable tabletop exercise from a checkbox activity.

## The takeaway

The value of a tabletop exercise isn't in a perfect performance — it's in finding the gaps in a plan before a real incident finds them for you.
