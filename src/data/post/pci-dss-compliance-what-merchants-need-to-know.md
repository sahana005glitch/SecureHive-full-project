PCI DSS applies to any organization that stores, processes, or transmits payment card data, with requirements that scale based on transaction volume.

## Key points

- Network segmentation isolating cardholder data environments significantly reduces the compliance scope and audit burden.
- Tokenization — replacing actual card numbers with a non-sensitive token — lets many merchants avoid handling raw card data at all.
- Non-compliance penalties are enforced by payment card brands and acquiring banks, not a government regulator, but can include fines and loss of card processing privileges.

## The takeaway

PCI DSS scope reduction (through segmentation and tokenization) is often the most cost-effective compliance strategy, rather than achieving full compliance across a wider environment.
