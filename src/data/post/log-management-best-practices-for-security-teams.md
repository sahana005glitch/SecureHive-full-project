Comprehensive, well-retained logs are often the difference between a fast, confident incident investigation and an inconclusive one.

## Key points

- Centralizing logs from all critical systems into one platform prevents an attacker who compromises a single system from also deleting the evidence of that compromise.
- Log retention periods should account for the fact that breaches are often discovered months after the initial compromise — overly short retention can erase crucial evidence.
- Time synchronization across all logging systems (via NTP) is a frequently overlooked prerequisite for accurately reconstructing an incident timeline.

## The takeaway

Good log management is invisible until the day you desperately need it — which is exactly why it has to be set up well before that day arrives.
