XDR extends EDR's endpoint-focused detection to correlate signals across endpoints, network, email, and cloud environments into a single view.

## Key points

- A key advantage of XDR is correlating a suspicious email with subsequent endpoint activity, connecting what would otherwise look like two unrelated alerts.
- XDR platforms aim to reduce alert fatigue by consolidating related signals into a single incident rather than dozens of disconnected alerts.
- The tradeoff is often vendor lock-in, since XDR platforms work best when most of an organization's security tools come from the same vendor ecosystem.

## The takeaway

XDR represents the industry's response to a real problem — security teams drowning in disconnected alerts from too many point solutions.
