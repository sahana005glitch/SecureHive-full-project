The CISO role has evolved from a primarily technical position into one that spans risk management, regulatory compliance, board communication, and technical strategy.

## Key points

- CISOs increasingly report business risk in financial and operational terms to boards, rather than purely technical metrics that don't translate to business impact.
- Balancing security investment against business velocity is a core CISO tension — overly restrictive controls can push employees toward shadow IT workarounds.
- Personal liability exposure for CISOs has increased following several high-profile cases, changing how the role approaches disclosure and documentation.

## The takeaway

The modern CISO role sits at the intersection of technology, risk, and business strategy — a far broader remit than the position's technical origins suggest.
