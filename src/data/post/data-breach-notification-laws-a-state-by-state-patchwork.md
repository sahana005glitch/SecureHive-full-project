Unlike GDPR's single EU-wide standard, U.S. data breach notification requirements vary significantly by state, creating compliance complexity for organizations operating nationally.

## Key points

- Notification timelines range from 'as expeditiously as possible' language to specific day counts (often 30-60 days), depending on the state.
- Definitions of what constitutes 'personal information' triggering notification requirements also vary — some states include biometric data, others don't.
- Organizations operating across multiple states often default to the strictest applicable requirement to simplify compliance rather than tracking each law separately.

## The takeaway

The patchwork nature of U.S. breach notification law is a genuine operational burden, which is part of why federal privacy legislation proposals keep resurfacing.
