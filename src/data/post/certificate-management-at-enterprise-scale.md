As organizations deploy more TLS certificates across more services, manual certificate management becomes both error-prone and a genuine operational risk.

## Key points

- Expired certificates are a surprisingly common cause of production outages, often because certificate renewal wasn't tracked centrally.
- Automated certificate lifecycle management tools handle issuance, renewal, and deployment without manual intervention, closing this operational gap.
- Short-lived certificates (a growing industry trend) reduce the impact of a compromised certificate, but require reliable automation to be practical at scale.

## The takeaway

Certificate management failures are almost always operational, not cryptographic — the fix is process and automation, not new technology.
