Passkeys, built on the FIDO2 standard, let users authenticate using their device's biometric or PIN, with no password ever transmitted or stored on the server.

## Key points

- Because passkeys are cryptographically bound to the specific site, they're inherently resistant to phishing in a way passwords never were.
- Major platforms (Apple, Google, Microsoft) now support passkey sync across devices, addressing an early adoption barrier around device loss.
- Enterprise passwordless rollout typically starts with high-value accounts before expanding, since it requires both server and client-side support.

## The takeaway

Passwordless authentication isn't just more convenient — it structurally eliminates entire categories of attack that have plagued passwords for decades.
