External vendors and contractors often need privileged access to specific systems, but managing that access with the same rigor as employee access is frequently overlooked.

## Key points

- Vendor access is often granted through shared credentials or standing VPN access, both of which make it difficult to attribute specific actions to a specific individual.
- Time-bound, session-recorded vendor access — granted only for the specific engagement window — significantly reduces third-party access risk.
- Automatically revoking vendor access at contract end (rather than relying on manual offboarding) closes a commonly exploited gap.

## The takeaway

Vendor access deserves the same least-privilege, time-bound treatment as any other privileged access — the fact that it's external doesn't reduce the risk.
