Patching sounds simple in principle and is consistently difficult in practice at enterprise scale, due to testing requirements and operational risk of change.

## Key points

- Staged rollout — patching a test group before full deployment — catches compatibility issues before they affect the whole organization.
- Critical security patches often warrant an expedited process outside the normal change management cycle, given the risk of delay versus the risk of a rushed change.
- Legacy systems that can't be patched (often due to vendor support end-of-life) require compensating controls like network isolation instead.

## The takeaway

The organizations that patch fastest aren't the ones with the most tools — they're the ones with the clearest, pre-agreed process for balancing patching speed against operational risk.
