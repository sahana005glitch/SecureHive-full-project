IGA answers a deceptively simple question at enterprise scale — who has access to what, and should they? — through structured policy, workflow, and periodic review.

## Key points

- Access certification campaigns periodically require managers to review and confirm their team's access is still appropriate, catching privilege creep.
- Role-based access control (RBAC) simplifies governance by tying access to job function rather than managing permissions individually per user.
- Automated provisioning and deprovisioning tied to HR systems ensures access is granted and revoked promptly as employees join, change roles, or leave.

## The takeaway

IGA turns access management from a reactive, ticket-driven process into a proactive, policy-driven one — which is exactly what auditors and attackers both notice the absence of.
