Security budgets are often the hardest to justify because success looks like nothing happening — a difficult story to tell in ROI terms.

## Key points

- Framing security investment in terms of risk reduction and potential breach cost avoidance resonates better with financial leadership than purely technical justifications.
- Benchmarking security spend as a percentage of IT budget against industry peers gives boards useful context for evaluating whether current spend is reasonable.
- Tying specific investments to specific, named risks (rather than generic 'improve security posture' asks) makes budget requests easier to evaluate and approve.

## The takeaway

Security budget conversations go better when framed as risk management decisions the business is making, not purely technical requests.
