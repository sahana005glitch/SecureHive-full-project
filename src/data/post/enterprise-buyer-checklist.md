Buying enterprise software is rarely just a feature comparison — security review, data residency, and integration effort usually decide the deal. Here's the checklist we give our own prospects.

## Security & compliance

- SOC 2 Type II report available on request
- Data residency options (US, EU, or both)
- SSO/SCIM support out of the box

## Integration effort

Ask vendors for a real architecture diagram, not a marketing one. If they can't show you where data flows, that's a signal.

## Support & SLAs

A 99.9% uptime SLA sounds impressive until you check the credit terms behind it. Read the fine print, or [ask us directly](/contact) — we're happy to walk through ours line by line.

## Total cost of ownership

Factor in implementation time and internal engineering hours, not just the license fee.
