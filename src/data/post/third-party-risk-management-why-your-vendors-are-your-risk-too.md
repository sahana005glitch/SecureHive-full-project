An organization's security posture is only as strong as its weakest connected vendor — a lesson repeatedly proven by supply-chain-adjacent breaches.

## Key points

- Vendor security questionnaires and SOC 2 report reviews are standard practice before granting a vendor access to sensitive systems or data.
- Continuous monitoring of vendor security posture (not just a one-time assessment at contract signing) catches risk that develops after onboarding.
- Limiting vendor access to exactly what's needed — and revoking it promptly when a contract ends — significantly reduces third-party exposure.

## The takeaway

Third-party risk isn't a one-time checkbox during procurement; it requires the same ongoing attention as internal security.
