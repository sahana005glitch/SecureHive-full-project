Attack surface management continuously discovers and monitors every externally-facing asset an organization has — including ones IT may not even know exist.

## Key points

- Shadow IT, forgotten subdomains, and assets from acquired companies are common sources of unknown attack surface that traditional asset inventories miss.
- Attackers routinely scan the entire internet for exposed, vulnerable services — an organization's unknown assets are still discoverable by adversaries even if unknown internally.
- Continuous, automated discovery (rather than periodic manual inventory) is necessary given how quickly cloud and DNS assets can be created and forgotten.

## The takeaway

You can't secure an asset you don't know exists — attack surface management exists specifically to close that visibility gap.
