Security teams often struggle to communicate program effectiveness — the right metrics can bridge the gap between technical work and business understanding.

## Key points

- Mean time to detect (MTTD) and mean time to respond (MTTR) are widely tracked metrics that directly reflect a security program's operational effectiveness.
- Vanity metrics (like raw number of blocked attacks) can be misleading, since they often reflect attack volume rather than actual security posture improvement.
- Patch compliance rates and percentage of assets covered by EDR are concrete, easily understood metrics that resonate with both technical and executive audiences.

## The takeaway

The best security metrics tell a story about risk reduction over time — not just activity volume, which can look impressive while meaning very little.
